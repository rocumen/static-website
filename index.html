Classification

Cloud Service Models

The three building blocks of cloud computing.

01

IaaS

Infrastructure as a Service — virtual machines, storage, and networking on demand.

  • Full control of IT resources
  • Pay-as-you-go pricing
  • Examples: AWS EC2, Google Compute Engine
02

PaaS

Platform as a Service — tools for app development without managing hardware.

  • Faster development cycles
  • Automated OS management
  • Examples: Google App Engine, Heroku
03

SaaS

Software as a Service — ready-to-use apps delivered over the Internet.

  • No maintenance needed
  • Accessible on any device
  • Examples: Google Workspace, Microsoft 365
