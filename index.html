Cloud Service Models

The three fundamental building blocks of modern cloud computing architectures.

🖥️

IaaS (Infrastructure as a Service)

Provides fundamental computing resources like virtual machines, storage, and networking on demand with complete control.

  • High-level control of IT resources
  • Pay-as-you-go infrastructure
  • Examples: AWS EC2, Google Compute Engine, Azure VMs
⚙️

PaaS (Platform as a Service)

Delivers hardware and software tools needed for application development without managing the underlying hardware.

  • Streamlined development cycles
  • Automated OS management & patching
  • Examples: Google App Engine, AWS Elastic Beanstalk, Heroku
📦

SaaS (Software as a Service)

Delivers end-user applications over the Internet managed entirely by the software vendor, accessible via web browsers.

  • Zero maintenance overhead
  • Accessible anywhere on any device
  • Examples: Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Dropbox

Deployment Models & Major Cloud Providers

How cloud environments are isolated, secured, and hosted across the globe.

Cloud Deployment Strategies

  1. Public Cloud: Owned and operated by third-party cloud providers, delivering computing resources over the public Internet.
  2. Private Cloud: Infrastructure used exclusively by a single business or organization, physically located on-premises or hosted by a third party.
  3. Hybrid Cloud: Combines public and private clouds, bound together by technology that allows data and applications to be shared.
  4. Multi-Cloud: The usage of two or more cloud computing services from different vendors to avoid vendor lock-in.

Why Cloud Computing Matters

Key business and technical drivers transforming contemporary computing.

01

Cost Efficiency

Eliminates capital expenses of buying hardware and software, running on-site data centers, and round-the-clock electricity.

02

Global Elasticity & Scaling

Allows elastic scaling of computing power, storage, and bandwidth right when it's needed from anywhere in the world.

03

Performance & Speed

Runs on worldwide networks of secure data centers regularly upgraded to the latest generation of fast, efficient computing hardware.

04

High Reliability & Redundancy

Data backup, disaster recovery, and business continuity are made easier and less expensive with multiple mirrored sites.
